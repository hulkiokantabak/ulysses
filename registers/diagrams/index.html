Every map, chart and panel

Maps, images and diagrams. Some stand alone as references in their own right; some do their work inside the PDF and HTML packages. One signature device per document (anchor A7).

  • The Difficulty Curve — 245 questions of one first reading, placed against the eighteen episodes — signature device: the difficulty curve
    Built from the Reading Companion, the project's primary record, 18 July 2026. Every quotation verbatim as spoken (anchor A6); n = 1 in the document's own voice (A10); the caveats are part of the document.
  • The Governed City — thirteen instruments of rule across the eighteen episodes — signature device: the matrix of instruments across the day
    Collected against the 1922 text (public domain, quoted freely) with hours from base document 03; adversarially verified, corrections disclosed in the document's caveats. The matrix marks presence per anchor A3, not weight.
  • The objects' day — every case's stations on one clock — signature device: the one-clock station chart
    Generated by tools/build-diagrams.mjs from the museum's own rows, 25 July 2026 — the same rows the build verifies against the 1922 text, so the figure cannot drift from the document (L-22). Hours approximate, from base document 03; the four unhoured stations are left off the clock rather than guessed onto it. States follow anchor A3.
  • Four Odysseys on two axes — fidelity to plot against interior orientation — signature device: the two-axis chart
    Drawn from the Four Odysseys handoff of 18 July 2026; re-founded 4 August 2026 on three plotted works. The film holds no placement: the reports about it are held in the register of reports on the chart page (record of record: data/nolan-reports.json), and the document's confidence markings survive into publication (anchor A5).
  • The Character Map — interactive (51 characters, four circles, 40 relationship threads) — signature device: the rings of the day
  • The Map and the Day — interactive (Dublin map, both routes, the 19-hour time rail) — signature device: the two routes and the time rail
  • The constraint graph — places as nodes, travel times as edges; a graph, not a map — signature device: the sourced-against-assumed graph
    Generated by tools/build-diagrams.mjs from the model's own NODES and EDGES arrays, 25 July 2026 — the data the operable model runs on, not a redrawing of it. Sourced and assumed edges are drawn apart (solid against dashed), because A5 holds in figures too. The circular arrangement is the diagram's only invention and means nothing.
  • What the record documents, and what it does not — signature device: the fifty-four-slot coverage grid
    Generated by tools/build-atlas-diagram.mjs from the source manifest, the derived slot targets, the recorded image choices and the fetched provenance, so the figure cannot drift from the state it draws (L-22). The gold ring is anchor A5 drawn: it marks a place whose subject base document 03 does not settle, where a person chose among several the record offers without ranking them. Episode titles are scaffolding, not Joyce's (A2).
