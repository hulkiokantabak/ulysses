EPISODE TITLES ARE SCHEMA SCAFFOLDING, NOT JOYCE'S — HE PRINTED NONE (ANCHOR A2).

This is not an episode companion. It is an index of the project's own work, set down where it falls in the day's text: the objects tracked through an episode, the women who enter there, the instruments of rule that mark it, the places where one first reading actually struggled. No entry summarises an episode. None carries reading guidance. Everything is derived from the dimensions and verified against the 1922 text at every build; empty is shown as empty.

What is not here, and why

Some dimensions, built and building, do not key to episodes. They are not forced to (contradictions and refusals are shown, not resolved — anchor A4):

  • The Concordance of Failures — keyed to Homeric episodes, not to the day's eighteen
  • The Constraint Model — keyed to hours and routes from base document 03, not to episodes
  • The Reading Log — the reader's own instrument; it collects nothing and cites nothing
  • Four Odysseys — compares whole works, not places in the day
