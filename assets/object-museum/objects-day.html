DIMENSION 1 · THE DIAGRAM REGISTER · EVERY CASE’S STATIONS ON ONE CLOCK · HOURS APPROXIMATE, FROM THE ITINERARY

Every object the museum tracks, laid on the one day. A lane is a case; a mark is a station at its approximate hour; the small hours after midnight sit at the day’s end, where the book puts them. A filled mark is an object present in the scene, an open mark one that lives there only in memory or talk, and a gold diamond one that appears in Circe’s visions and dreams — the three states the museum keeps strictly apart, kept apart here too.
