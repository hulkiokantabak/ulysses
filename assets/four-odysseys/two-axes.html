DISSENT — SARAMAGO, AT FULL STRENGTH, beside the register and not beneath it. A separately-denominated register is "the same evasion at one remove" — the project keeping the column while appearing to give it up. The remedy is removal: strip the film of any axis position until the project holds it. The warrant: "a project whose entire authority is that it says what it does not have must be strictest exactly where it is being invited to be precise." Recorded with it, the tripwire, in the session's words: "If the register drifts back toward parity of layout, he was right and the finding was a compromise."

The four-instruments finding

Published on this register per the chair's resolution of the author's item what-is-owed-the-twelve, clause (c), with the correction the verifier required. At the hanging of the twelve enslaved women, four instruments fail in the same direction: Homer counts the twelve and does not name them. Wilson's stated policy fails to reach her hardest scene — she writes 'these girls' at the hanging — and the refusal of a clean death is, in her rendering, TELEMACHUS's sentence, a killer's stated cruelty and not a translator's comment on her own practice. The reported film elides the hanging onto a single betrayer — reported, second-hand, permanently. And dimension 2's census could not reach the unnamed until the register of women drawn and never named was built. The blind spot is at the same coordinate every time: the woman who is owned, counted, and not named.

DISSENT — SARAMAGO, AT FULL STRENGTH, beside this finding and not beneath it. The three-way comparison should not be published in any form. Comparing claims still seats a rumour on an ordinal beside two printed texts, and no label drains precision back out of a placed object. Strip the Nolan column of any position, not merely of movement.
