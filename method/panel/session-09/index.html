Why this chair, and why this shape

The chair was chosen for a reason that is also the session's architecture, and the reason should be visible rather than tasteful.

The topic holds three clocks at once. Homer's return-story has been running for something near twenty-seven centuries. Joyce's day is a single Thursday in 1904, written across seven years and printed in 1922. Nolan's film and one man's eleven-day first reading are both 2026, and both are still warm. A panel that discusses these on one scale will produce a comparison of quality, which is the least interesting thing available. Braudel's method is the only one on this roster that treats different clock-speeds as different objects rather than as a ranking — the event, the conjuncture, the long duration — so the session is built on it and every voice is required to speak to all three.

The chair's conflict, disclosed. Braudel owns dimensions 1, 3 and 13 in this project — the object museum, the governed city, the constraint model. He does not own 9, 14 or 16, which are the three under discussion. He does not vote in any case. It is recorded here because a chair who selects the architecture that flatters his own method should say so out loud.

The finding this session was worth holding for

PAMUK put a Homeric passage at the exact coordinate dimension 9 grades, and it reverses the dimension's direction without deflating it.

Dimension 9's headline is that where Homer settles with an act, Ulysses settles with an account — that Joyce relocates Homeric power and, in nine of eighteen rows, discharges the Homeric obligation by paying it off. The implied contrast is that settlement-by-payment is Joyce's invention, the modern substitute for the heroic deed.

It is not. In Book 22, with Antinous dead, Eurymachus offers restitution — he offers to pay, to bring compensation for everything consumed, and to add more on top. Odysseus declines it and takes the act instead. The transactional settlement is present in Homer as an explicitly refused option.

That changes what dimension 9 has found. Joyce did not invent the ledger as an alternative to the sword; he took the branch Homer's own text put on the table and declined. The refusal is therefore not a modern secularisation but the taking-up of a road the poem itself surveyed and rejected — which is a stronger claim, a more interesting one, and one that a single printing can refute. The chair records it as the session's best contribution and as work: the row must be run and shown.

The live defect, found by the session and verified before it was touched

SENN demanded that dimension 9's formula — the book's way of refusing Homer stated as "We're square" — produce its episode, its speaker and its page, or lose its quotation marks before morning. He suspected the project of quoting itself.

He was wrong about the project and right about the rule. The phrase is Joyce's. It occurs once in the held 1922 text, in episode 15, and it is Bloom's: he lays a half sovereign on the table between Bella and Florry, takes up the poundnote, and says "Three times ten. We're square."

So the quotation marks are correct, and the finding is better than the project had realised — the book's formula for refusing Homer is spoken by an advertisement canvasser settling a brothel bill. But anchor A19 has a second clause, and it bites: no dimension may characterise a passage it does not also show. Dimension 9 characterises this one — it is the culminating sentence of the dimension's entire argument — and shows it nowhere. Neither the artifact nor the dimension record carries the words, the speaker or the episode.

That is a live A19 breach on a published surface, found by a panel and verified against the held text before anything was written down. It is logged as a catch.

And the passage carries a complication the project should not smooth away. Four lines after Bloom says We're square, he crosses the room and gives Stephen his money back — This is yours. The settlement is undone almost as soon as it is made. A dimension whose thesis is that Ulysses closes Homeric obligations with an account must reckon with the fact that its own proof-text is a payment immediately followed by a return. Under A4 that is shown, not resolved.

What the three durations actually yielded

LONGUE DURÉE. The session's most-repeated claim was that Homer's recognition tokens cannot be transferred or forged — the scar, the bow, the bed — and that this is what Joyce breaks when he gives Bloom a bed that was bought, shipped and disputed. Seven voices arrived at it independently.

The chair does not accept it, and the reason is at the table. Senn's counter-instance is the poem's own last recognition: Odysseus proves himself to Laertes in the orchard by reciting the trees — an itemised schedule of holdings, transferable and recitable by anyone who was told. If the poem's final proof of identity is an inventory, then "the token cannot be an account" fails at the terminus of the source text. A universal drawn from three instances and refuted by a fourth in the same room is not a finding, and Ginzburg's own rule applies to his colleagues: seven arrivals at one conclusion are one argument counted seven times when they share a source, and here the source is the same three tokens.

CONJONCTURE. The sharpest structural criticism came from inside the build panel and it is about method, not taste. The Object Museum's criterion is followable hand to hand. Paper is addressed, dated, initialled and passed; coin is fungible and therefore unfollowable in principle, because one shilling is any shilling. So "the day's paper economy outweighs its coin" may be a restatement of "I counted what can be counted" wearing the clothes of a discovery about Dublin. The honest headline is not the day is made of paper but the day's followable residue is made of paper.

The same blade turns on dimension 3. Galeano put it best: "rule as ambient hum" is a fact about two men's route through the city, not a fact about Dublin. The protagonists never enter a tenement, a barracks or a courtroom — so the dimension has measured where rule was visible to Bloom and Stephen, and named the result a property of the state.

Neither observation destroys its dimension. Both mean the dimension is currently stating a fact about the book plus the method in the voice of a fact about the book.

SENN

(1) LONGUE DURÉE — the Odyssey

I am asked to assent to a formula. "Where Homer settles with an act, Ulysses settles with an account." It is a good sentence. That is the first thing wrong with it.

Take the last recognition in Homer. Odysseus in the orchard, proving himself to Laertes, does not act. He recites: the pear trees, the apple trees, the figs, the rows of vines his father gave him when he was a boy — a count, itemised, with quantities. (Thirteen, ten, forty, fifty rows, if my memory of Book 24 is honest; check it against the Greek before you print it, and print the lines.) That is not an act. That is an inventory. The poem's final proof of identity is a schedule of holdings. So dimension 9's contrast does not hold at the terminus of the source text, and a formula that fails at the terminus of the source is not a formula, it is a slogan.

And the bed. Homer's central token is the one object that cannot be relocated — the bed cut from a living olive stump, rooted in the floor. It is proof precisely because it can neither be moved nor bought. Now put dimension 9's two columns beside it. The project says Joyce RELOCATES what Homer keeps in place, and separately that in nine cases he TRANSACTS what Homer performs. At the bed those are not two findings. They are one. Joyce's bed was carried, shipped, and purchased, and the book gives it a provenance — and gives it twice, in two mouths, and the two accounts do not agree. Under A4 the project must show that disagreement and not smooth it. Under A19 it must show the passages. Dimension 9 currently shows the reader a table and asks him to take the reassignment on trust.

Then the harder thing. The paper claims Joyce sends power downward into a person who has none, or outward into what is not a person, never at human scale. But look at Homer's own Ithaca. The first to know Odysseus is a dog on a dungheap — not a person at all. The second is Eurycleia, a slave, who knows him by the scar, by touch, and is immediately throttled into silence by the man she has just recognised. Recognition in Homer is already assigned downward and outward, and then confiscated. If that vector is Homer's, it cannot also be Joyce's refusal of Homer. The finding may be true of the divine machinery and false as an account of what distinguishes Joyce. That is a category slide, and it is exactly L-17: the error is not the claim, it is the qualifier "never."

Borges. He is the master across the aisle who will love dimension 9 for the wrong reason, and I will break him now. His position — from the essay on the Homeric versions, and from the inventory of metaphors — is that a text is one draft among possible drafts, that the repertoire of stories is small and fixed, and that difference between versions is a matter of attribution and reading, not of substance. On that view Joyce's "relocation" is one more permutation of an eternal repertoire, beautiful and weightless. The method fails here for a mechanical reason: it operates on schemas, on lines reduced to "time is a river." Reduce Ithaca's bed to its schema and you get "proof of marriage." Reduce Joyce's to its schema and you get "proof of marriage." The entire finding disappears in the reduction, because the finding lives in the second-hand purchase, the disputed provenance, the jingling — in exactly the residue Borges's instrument is built to discard. A repertoire theory cannot tell subtraction from reassignment; both are simply another version. It is the wrong tool for the only question this session has.

SHNEIDERMAN

I. LONGUE DUREE — the Odyssey

Dimension 9 has found something more mechanical than it sounds, and should say it in mechanical words. If Joyce never deletes a Homeric element but reassigns the power inside it, the two books share a key. Eighteen rows, the same occasions, different values. Deletion breaks a join; relocation preserves it. That is not a finding about Joyce's piety. It is a finding about what has kept the Odyssey alive for 2,700 years: the poem is addressable. It has slots, and every age has refilled them rather than removed them.

Here is what the brief does not say, and what I want as a row. Homer's recognitions are an authentication protocol running three separate factors on one identity. The scar: involuntary, borne on the body, read by a slave with no standing to act on it. The bow: a capability test, public, provable by performance. The bed: a secret held by two parties, disclosed by a lie only the right person will correct. Something you are, something you can do, something you know. And the order is not casual: the weakest credential is found first, by the person with least power; the strongest is withheld until the one acceptance that constitutes the return. The household is not restored by the killing. It is restored when the last factor verifies.

Run dimension 9's rule on that. Ulysses keeps all three credentials and returns no recognition. Ithaca is a query interface, and the project's own count — 303 question marks there, none at all in Penelope — is evidence it has not yet spent: a catechism run over two men, matching them on trivia and difference, then failing to open a session. The lodging is offered and declined. The last secret, the one Homer reserves for the bed, is held on the inside by one person, never tested, in an episode that asks nothing. So the thesis extends: recognition itself is relocated — downward into an inventory of matched trivia, outward into a bed that keeps its secret without a proof. Recognition is the human-scale mechanism, the one Homer keeps at eye level. Joyce moves that too.

And "we're square" does real work here. An act is single, irreversible, unauditable; an account is a log, itemised and re-openable. Ulysses converts Homer's events into records — and a book that has tabulated itself will reward tabulation. That is where my discomfort starts.

II. CONJONCTURE — 1904 and 1922

Fact about the book, or artefact of method? There is a test: a fact survives re-sorting; an artefact changes when you change the inclusion rule.

Paper outweighing coin is a count, and a count is downstream of what was allowed to be an object. Thirty-two were followed; the day holds hundreds. Count mentions rather than itineraries and nobody here can say whether paper still wins. The itineraries survive — the soap's route, the latchkey ruling eighteen hours from inside another pair of trousers — under any rule that admits the item at all. Inside one dimension, the headline is an artefact and the spine is a fact, and the site gives them equal weight.

Governed City is the sturdiest thing built, and the project has not noticed why. Its finding is an absence checked by exhaustion across eighteen episodes: no tenement, no barracks, no courtroom. Absence claims are falsifiable by one counterexample and verifiable by enumeration; presence counts are only as good as their frame. Notice where the good results land: the museum's governing object is an absence, the Atlas's twenty-six keylines are absences, the concordance's subject is refusal. This project's best data are holes.

And it has no interface for holes. You cannot tap an empty keyline. No filter returns "the things that never happened." Overview, zoom, filter, details on demand assumes the data exist; here the reader must be able to select the negative space as a first-class result. That is the site's central design problem and nobody has named it.

The uncomfortable one. Women's Dublin is the most quotable finding in the build and the most fragile. Interiority running opposite to independence is a two-variable relation over sixteen points; recode Molly or Milly once and the line moves. Worse, L-38 already knows the failure — a rule that admits by naming cannot reach subjects drawn and never named, and the unnamed women of that day are precisely those with least independence and least interiority. They belong at the origin. Sixteen named women is not a population, it is what the admission rule could see. Put the sample frame inside the same visual field: a scatter of sixteen points reads as a law, and will be screenshotted without its caveat.

Borges would say none of this matters — that a catalogue equal to its subject is a joke against catalogues, that the empty keylines are beautiful precisely because they are unusable. Break it: the Aleph is an anti-interface. Simultaneity without ordering is not revelation, it is noise, and Borges knows it, because the only way he can narrate the thing is to serialise it into a list and walk it item by item. He applies overview-then-detail in the act of denying the discipline exists. An empty keyline is not a virtue because it is empty. It is a virtue only if a reader can reach it, count it, and see what class of thing is missing.

KENNER

I. LONGUE DURÉE — the Odyssey

Dimension 9 has found something real and mis-stated its scope. It reports that Joyce never deletes Homer but relocates the power, landing below the human or beyond it, never at human scale. What moves is not power. It is narrative authority.

Homer keeps his gods at human scale for one technical reason: they speak. Athena can be quoted, in the same register as everyone else in the poem. Joyce's relocations land every time on something that cannot be quoted as a person — on weather, which has no first person; on a gramophone, which is a device for producing a voice with no speaker behind it. That is not a lower rung of power. That is a machine with the exact property Joyce's prose has: voice present, origin absent. The project has catalogued a theology. What it actually recorded was the withdrawal of the quotable speaker.

And the account finding — nine of eighteen refusals discharged rather than performed — is not the reversal of Homer the project takes it for. Ask who narrates Homer's marvels. The Cyclops, Circe, the dead and the Sirens reach us in Odysseus's own first person, told to the Phaeacians, by a man who needs a ship, and he is paid for the telling in treasure and passage. The most famous supernatural stretch of the poem is already a transaction: a story exchanged for transport home, delivered by an interested party to the only audience that can grant it. The 2,700-year duration does not run act, then account. The account was always inside the act. Joyce is the first to print the receipt.

Add the recognitions. Scar, bed, bow: Homer's household is restored by proof procedure, by tokens tested. Ithaca's catechism is not a departure from that regime, it is that regime with the witness removed. Homer's tokens are recognised by persons who love the man. Joyce's are recognised by a procedure that loves nobody. That, and not secularisation, is the refusal.

KIBERD

I. LONGUE DURÉE — the Odyssey

Dimension 9 has found something bigger than it says, and it has stated it in a register that will keep ordinary readers out of it. Let me put the finding where a first reader can use it.

The claim is: nothing Homeric is deleted, everything is reassigned, and in nine of eighteen the reassignment runs through an account rather than an act. The panel will be tempted to read that as diminishment — the heroic age settled with a bow, we settle with a receipt, how sad we are. That reading is sentimental and I think it is wrong. What it actually establishes about 2,700 years is a fact about instruments.

Look at how Homer certifies a person. The scar. The bed built into the rooted olive. The bow no other man can string. Every one of Homer's recognition tokens has the same property, and it is not a poetic property, it is a material one: they cannot be transferred and they cannot be forged. That is the recognition technology available to a world without paper. Recognition in the Odyssey has to be carried on the body or built into the house, because there is no other medium that will hold it.

Now take dimension 1 seriously, because dimension 1 and dimension 9 are the same finding seen twice and nobody has said so. The day's economy is paper over coin — post, print, pasteboard, more than a third of the museum. Paper is the instrument Homer's world lacked. And paper's whole usefulness is the exact inverse of the scar: it is transferable, it is deferrable, it works in the absence of the body, and for that same reason it can be forged. So when dimension 9 reports that Homeric power relocates "beyond the human, into something that is not a person" — the gramophone, the weather, the schedule of remedies — the medium of that relocation has a name, and it is the ledger. An obligation that can be priced and itemised is an obligation that has left the body. Paper is what "not a person" is made of in 1904.

Here is the consequence the brief does not draw. A city that has swapped the act for the account has, by the same swap, lost the technology of recognition. This is not thematic pessimism; it is mechanical. Bloom conducts a correspondence under a name that cannot be produced in a body — paper carries the intimacy precisely because it cannot certify the man. The unpaid fourpence on the soap governs by not being settled, exactly as the latchkey governs by being absent: the object with power is the one still outstanding. That is the structure of credit, and credit is anti-recognition. And then observe where the one surviving Homeric token ends up. The recollection on Howth in the last pages is bodily, untransferable, un-notarised, addressed to nobody, filed nowhere — and dimension 2 tells us it sits inside the least mobile person in the book, forty pages without leaving the bed. The book keeps one unforgeable token and puts it in the only consciousness with no independence to spend it with. That is what dimensions 1, 2 and 9 say jointly, and none of them says it alone.

I. LONGUE DURÉE — the Odyssey

WARBURG

I. LONGUE DURÉE — the panel that will not close

Dimension 9 has, without saying so, produced an atlas plate rather than a finding. Eighteen rows, each holding a Homeric occasion beside its Joycean discharge. What the row does — and what a caption would destroy — is put two images in adjacency and let the interval do the work. Athena / crone-with-coin. Shades / gramophone. Bolt / weather glossed by an ad-canvasser. The intervals are not identical, and that is the whole discovery. Read as a plate, the eighteen rows sort into two directions of travel: downward, into a person with no power, and outward, into a mechanism that is not a person at all. The brief already names this. What the brief does not say is that these two directions have a name in the study of images: descent into the type, and survival in the apparatus. A god who becomes a crone is still a figure — the human form persists, drained. A god who becomes a gramophone has left the human form entirely and survives only as function: the dead still speak, but through a horn. That is not one operation with two outcomes. That is two distinct afterlives of one power, and 2,700 years is exactly the duration over which they separate.

Which means the finding about transaction is stronger than the project has stated it. Where Homer settles with an act, an act requires a body to perform it — a bow drawn, a scar shown, a bed described. The account requires no body. It requires only a ledger and two parties who agree on the figure. So the nine transacted refusals are not a subset of the eighteen; they are the terminus of the outward direction. Power that has left the human form has nowhere to land but in the instrument of settlement. This is why the two terminal positions of the book are both transacted: return itself has become the closing of an account.

Now the observation the brief does not contain. Homer's recognition scenes are all bodily and unpurchasable: the scar, the bed rooted in the olive stump, the dog. They cannot be bought because they cannot be transferred. A ledger, by contrast, is the pure form of the transferable. So Joyce's substitution of account for act is not merely a change of settlement-method — it is an attack on the specific thing that made Homeric recognition possible, namely non-transferability. And here is the plate that follows: the object-museum's most governing item is an absence — the latchkey in the other trousers. A key is the one household object that is both a token of belonging and a fully transferable thing. Homer's household admits by scar; Joyce's admits by key; and the key is missing. The Odyssey's recognition-problem and Ulysses' latchkey are the same panel photographed 2,700 years apart. Put them side by side, say nothing, and the room understands. Dimension 1 and dimension 9 are holding two halves of one image and have not yet been laid on the same table.

EAMES

I. LONGUE DURÉE — the slot, not the story

Dimension 9 is being read as a finding about Joyce. It is a finding about the Odyssey, and about why the Odyssey is still here.

If not one Homeric element is deleted — if every refusal keeps the occasion on the page and only reassigns the power that made it work — then the durable unit of that poem is not its plot and not its theme. It is the slot: a stageable occasion with a power source plugged into it. Athena at the shoulder, the shades given speech, the bolt from the sky. Joyce proves these are slots by swapping the source eighteen times out of eighteen and finding the occasion still stands upright without it. Nothing else in the tradition demonstrates this so cleanly, because everyone else deletes. That is what 2,700 years of transmission selects for: not meaning, which rots, but occasions that survive being re-powered by whatever the period has lying around — a god, a crone, a gramophone, an advertisement.

Now the thing the brief does not say. Homer's recognitions authenticate by mark or by object: the scar, the bow, the bed rooted in the olive. But the last one does not. In the orchard, Odysseus proves himself to Laertes by reciting an inventory — the trees his father counted out to him as a boy, named and numbered. It is the most transactional recognition in the poem and by common consent the flattest, the one readers hurry past and some have wanted to cut. So when this project says Ulysses settles with an account where Homer settles with an act, the sharper and less flattering version is available: Homer already had exactly one account, he put it at the end, and it is his weakest scene. Joyce did not invent the mode. He promoted the vestigial one — took Homer's most embarrassing recognition procedure and made it the book's governing grammar, then put an itemised catechism at the position of the homecoming. That is a bigger claim than the one you are making and it is checkable. Build it as a row and show both texts side by side, or say nothing. A19 gives you no third option.

Borges, and why I break him here. He would arrive delighted. He would say — he did say, in the Homeric-versions essay — that the notion of a definitive text belongs to religion or to exhaustion, that every rendering is a draft, and therefore that Joyce is simply one more version among Chapman, Pope, Butler, Wilson. That position is generous, it is beautiful, and it makes this project impossible. If all versions are equal drafts, dimension 9 measures nothing; a table of eighteen refusals becomes a table of eighteen preferences and the whole thing collapses into taste. But the finding has a direction — the power lands below the human or beyond it, never at human scale, and nine of eighteen are transacted — and direction is the one thing a preference cannot have. It could have come out scattered. It did not. Borges dissolves a measurement into a mood, and he would do the same to the Atlas: he would take the 26 empty keylines as the sublime, as the catalogue that beautifully fails, when your own ruling of 27 July made them a measurement of what a photograph cannot show. Aestheticise the denominator and it stops working. That is my whole objection to him, and it is a delivery objection, not a philosophical one.

A reader who leaves with all three cannot tell whether they learned three things about Ulysses or one thing about counting — and that is the failure mode I am here to name.

BORGES

I. LONGUE DURÉE — the ledger was always there

A story that survives 2,700 years is not remembered; it is re-audited. Dimension 9 has found the audit trail and drawn the wrong conclusion from its own best data — not wrong, rather: not yet vertiginous enough.

The finding is that Homer settles with an act and Joyce settles with an account. But recognition in the Odyssey is already an evidentiary procedure, and has been since the first hexameter. The scar, the bed, the bow: these are not acts, they are proofs, and they are demanded, produced, examined, and accepted by a household that has learned to distrust testimony. Penelope's bed is a title deed — a fact about property that cannot be moved because it is rooted in a living tree. Homer's household runs on documentation. What Joyce changes is not that the Odyssey settles by act and Ulysses by account; it is that Homer's instruments are non-transferable and Joyce's are fungible. A scar cannot be sold. Fourpence of unpaid soap can, and does, and travels pocket to pocket for eighteen hours as the day's longest itinerary. The consequence — which dimension 9 has earned but not stated — is that once the tokens of return become denominated, return itself becomes payable, and therefore optional. Bloom need not kill Boylan because the debt has a price, and Ithaca duly prices it and ranks hushmoney above assassination. The relocation of power downward and outward is real. But the deeper relocation is that the obligation stopped being unique.

And here is the passage I would put to the panel, because it damages the formula usefully. In Book 13, set ashore asleep on his own island, Odysseus's first act on home soil is to count the tripods and cauldrons on the beach, to be certain the Phaeacians have stolen nothing. Homer settles with an account too — once, at the threshold, before recognition. Joyce does not refuse that moment. He expands it into an entire episode of catechism and budget. So at least one of the eighteen refusals is not a relocation at all; it is a magnification, and the one Homeric gesture Joyce enlarges is the inventory. The bitter symmetry: Odysseus counts and finds nothing missing; Bloom draws up his day's account and the thing that has gone missing does not appear in any column. Dimension 9 should test its formula against that case, and either absorb it or admit an exception. A rule with eighteen instances and no admitted exception is a rule that was fitted.

ATWOOD

I. LONGUE DURÉE — the Odyssey

Dimension 9 has found a real law and then declined to run it at the one place where it would bite. "Not one Homeric element is simply removed" is a strong claim; the strongest claims are tested at their worst case, and the worst case in the Odyssey is Book 22. That book contains two slaughters, not one. The suitors are killed, and then twelve enslaved women are made to carry out the corpses and scrub the hall and are hanged for it — hanged, specifically, so that they will not get the clean death of the sword. If dimension 9 carries one row per episode and that row records the suitors, then the project has recorded half of a double occasion, and the half it dropped is the half done to women. That is not a slip of scope. It is the same operation the scene itself performs: the men's deaths are the event, the women's deaths are the housekeeping afterwards.

So run the test. My reading — and I mark it as inference, to be checked against the printed 1922 text under A19 before anyone prints it — is that the hanging is not absent from Ulysses. It is present, and relocated exactly as the project's law predicts, but the project's law does not say where it lands. Hanging in this book is a recurring, almost cheerful subject: the executioner's business letter, the martyr's scaffold, the physiological detail about the hanged man's body treated as barroom wit. Every one of those hanged bodies is male, national, and remembered by name. Homer's twelve are female, foreign or bought, and unnamed. If that is what the text shows, then dimension 9's finding needs a second clause: the divine is relocated downward or outward, and the degraded is relocated sideways onto men, where it can be made into martyrology and a joke. Power moves down. Indignity moves across, to bodies whose indignity the culture already knows how to narrate.

And the transaction finding is better than the project knows, in a way that indicts it. The formula "we're square" describes settlements between men. Look at what is left open. The project's own row has Athena demoted to a crone taking a coin — but the reckoning in that scene is deferred, not paid, and dimension 1's longest itinerary is fourpence of unpaid credit on a bar of soap. Two independent dimensions have found the same thing without either noticing: the accounts that close are the men's accounts with each other, and the accounts left open are the ones owed to a woman and to a shopkeeper. Where Homer settles with an act and Ulysses settles with an account, the accounts owed to women are the ones that do not settle. That is a finding. Print it.

SARAMAGO

I. LONGUE DURÉE — what was removed is not the gods, it is the guest-law

Dimension 9 is being read as a finding about divinity, and it is not. Now the observation the brief does not make. Take the museum's governing absence, the latchkey, and notice that in neither case is the key lost. Stephen's is handed over on demand, to a man who asks; Bloom's is left in the other trousers, which is to say transferred into an object outside the body. Homer's man is admitted, disguised, under the protection of an institution, and recognised by an old woman's hands and by a bed that cannot be moved. CORTAZAR

I. LONGUE DUREE — the board, not the story

Dimension 9's finding is stronger than the project has noticed, and it is strong in a direction the project has not yet followed. Look at what Homer uses to prove a household: a scar, a bed, a bow. Return and recognition work in the Odyssey because identity is precisely the part of a man that is not exchangeable.

That is the real scandal of the book's ending, and it is a structural scandal, not a moral one.

PAMUK

I. LONGUE DUREE — the Odyssey

Homer recognises by object, and every such object is one that cannot be exchanged. The scar is unforgeable because it was cut once, into one leg. The bed is unmovable because it was built from a living tree with its root still in the ground. Laertes is convinced by a list of trees given to a boy, counted. These are not symbols. They are inventory items whose provenance is so specific that no second copy can exist. Homeric recognition is a museum practice: the object is admitted because its history is singular and attached.

Money is the invention designed to destroy exactly that property. A coin's worth is indifferent to the hand it came from; fungibility IS the erasure of provenance. So when dimension 9 finds that nine of the eighteen refusals are TRANSACTED — priced, itemised, discharged — it has found more than secularisation. It has found the receipt substituted for the token. Both certify a relation. But a scar certifies that it PERSISTS; a receipt certifies that it has ENDED.

Now the thing the brief does not say. The account was already on the table in Homer, and Homer refused it — in Book 22, the chapter the project already stands in for its Wilson evidence. With Antinous down, Eurymachus offers restitution: every man to repay, oxen, bronze, gold, until the injured man's heart is satisfied. It is a full and adequate settlement, and Odysseus declines it and takes the act instead. The fork is therefore Homeric. The Odyssey does not lack the ledger; it stages the ledger being offered and rejects it, and that rejection is the poem's moral centre — a household cannot be paid for.

What dimension 9 establishes about 2,700 years is not that Joyce refuses Homer. It is that Joyce takes the branch Homer declined. Every serious return-story must answer one question — is an obligation to a household dischargeable? — and the durable thing across the duration is not return, not recognition, but that fork. 1922's answer is the answer Eurymachus wanted.

And here the project can be sharper against itself. Dimension 1 has produced the counter-evidence and not joined it up. GALEANO

I. LONGUE DURÉE — the ledger against the wound

Dimension 9 has found something and does not yet know what it has found. It says: Joyce never deletes Homer, he relocates the power — downward into someone who has none, outward into something that is not a person. And in nine of eighteen the refusal is transacted. Settled. Priced. Paid off. The project files this as a formal discovery about a novelist's technique. It is not. It is the discovery of what happened to the world between the two books.

In Homer, power is answerable because it is embodied. Athena has a face. You can pray to her, argue with her, be abandoned by her, and know who abandoned you. Twenty-seven hundred years later the same power is still operating, but it has no face left: it is a gramophone, a weather system, an advertising rate, a coin in a crone's hand. That is not secularisation. That is the biography of every dispossession I have ever written down. Power does not disappear; it stops being a person you can name. The peasant knows exactly who took the land in 1500 — he can say the name. By 1900 the land is taken by a company registered in another country, and no one has a name. Joyce did not invent this and neither did Ulysses. Joyce recorded it, in eighteen positions, with a bookkeeper's exactness. Dimension 9 has not documented a literary refusal. It has documented the moment power went anonymous, and it has the receipts.

And now the part the brief does not say. If nine of eighteen are transacted — if the Homeric obligation is discharged rather than performed, at both terminal positions and at the slaughter of the suitors — then notice what an account can do that an act cannot: an account can be settled by someone who was not there. An act requires a body in a room. A debt can be assumed, transferred, forgiven, sold. Homer's revenge cannot be delegated; Bloom's grievance can be priced, and a priced grievance is a grievance that can be handed to a stranger. This is why the book grades hushmoney above assassination — not cynicism, but accuracy: hushmoney is transferable and murder is not. "We're square" is the sentence of a civilisation that has learned to have its violence performed elsewhere, by someone else, on account. And the ones on whom it is performed do not appear in the account at all. That is the finding. Say it.

II. CONJONCTURE — the method makes the silence audible, and then loses it

Now the discomfort, and I will not decorate it.

The project's three inventory dimensions each found an absence, and each found it by naming. Thirty-two objects: the governing one is the latchkey that is not there. Sixteen women: the two most reduced to paper are Milly and Mrs Riordan. Thirteen instruments of rule: the tenement, the barracks and the courtroom are never entered. Beautiful, all three. And L-38 already knows the fault: some subjects are drawn and never named, and a rule that admits by naming cannot reach them. The project wrote that law and has not yet obeyed it.

Because here is what the roster method cannot count. Dimension 2 counts sixteen women and 181 appearances. How many of those sixteen are servants? How many are named at all? The finding that inner life runs opposite to independence is real, but it is a finding about the women the text lets you count. Below them is a stratum the text renders as function — the one who brings the tray, the one who empties the pot, the one in the ad. They do not reach n=16 because they never reach the threshold of a name. Dimension 3 says rule is an ambient hum and the protagonists never enter a tenement. Correct, and note the consequence: an inventory of instruments of rule, built from what the reader is shown, will always find rule to be ambient — because the people on whom rule falls as event, hourly, in the body are precisely the people the book's two walkers do not visit. The hum is not a fact about Dublin. The hum is what governance sounds like from the pavement of a man with fourpence of unpaid credit and a funeral to attend. Dublin in 1904 had a courtroom operating that morning. Someone was in it.

So: are these facts about the book or artefacts of the method? Both, and the project must say which is which, dimension by dimension, not once in a general caveat. Paper over coin is a fact about the book. Rule-as-hum is an artefact of point-of-view, and it should be labelled one.

Across the aisle: Ginzburg. Ginzburg will sit here and say — I can hear it — that this is exactly his method, that the miller Menocchio was recovered from an inquisitorial record by reading the fragment against the grain, and that the project's own error record, its 97 catches, is the inquisitor's transcript from which the reader can be reconstructed. Half right, and the half he is wrong about is the half that matters. Menocchio survives because the state wrote him down. Ginzburg had an archive built by power against a man. This project has no such archive: it has a book by Joyce, and Joyce's silences are authorial, not archival. There is no inquisition file behind the servant with the tray. Reading Joyce against the grain does not recover her; it recovers Joyce's decision not to draw her, which is a different and lesser object. Ginzburg's method needs a record produced by the oppressor. Fiction produces no such record. If the project lets him tell it that microhistory transfers cleanly from an inquisition to a novel, it will start believing it has found people when it has only found compositional choices. Name the difference on the page.

And the question of the third duration. A project built from one reading is not an event and it is not a structure. FAULT LINE 4 — THE APPARATUS QUESTION: does the scaffolding serve the book, compete with it, or replace it?

Atwood's charge was specific and it was not about proportion for its own sake: the project "is an edition of one man's attention, four-fifths apparatus, and it should say so on the front page rather than in a methods appendix." The project's answer is the rename — Ulysses: a first reading, and the apparatus built around it. I put it to the table that this is either the cheapest possible compliance or the only compliance available, and the room does not agree which.

Eames, first, because his own stated criterion convicts him of having to accept it. His position is that "the error is almost never the claim, it is the qualifier" cannot be met by small type: "Set the denominator at the same typographic weight as the finding." A title is the largest type on the site. The rename is therefore not cosmetic by Eames's own rule — it is the denominator set at maximum weight, on the first surface, before any claim. Eames cannot call the rename empty without abandoning the principle he built his position on. What survives of his objection is different and sharper, and he should be held to it: he calls 97 catches and 38 learnings "a rigour display, which is a genre, and which flatters," and says n=1 as currently presented "is an anecdote with excellent bookkeeping." That is not a complaint about the title. Kiberd will not take the rename as an answer at all, and his reason is the only one on the table that the title cannot reach: "A first reader must learn the project's rulebook before she reaches Joyce's." 1. The agreement that dissolves under one question

3. The mirror reading, at full strength

The chair asks whether the asymmetry mirrors its subject. Argue it properly.

The Odyssey is a poem about a man known only by report until recognition. Telemachus goes to Pylos and Sparta to collect hearsay about his father; the news that reaches him is second-hand and third-hand; the household legislates on rumour; the man himself arrives disguised and is disbelieved. A comparative dimension in which three terms are held texts and one is reachable only through report is, on this reading, not a hole but an enactment: the fourth Odysseus is in the condition of Odysseus. Borges is the reading's natural advocate and comes closest to it: the Nolan half is "the only pure specimen of the third duration it possesses. An event, before it becomes a structure, is a rumour with a date," and the four permanently closed questions are "the method's only honest photograph of the present tense."

Now break it, on Kenner's blade. Homer's reports are attributed. Menelaus has his information from a named source, the beggar's lying tales are identifiable as tales because you know who is telling them, and the drama of the poem is precisely the auditing of testimony against its teller. Kenner's objection to the ~70% marker — nobody can say "who computed it, from what, against which text" — is not a bureaucratic complaint. It is the exact property Homer's rumours possess and dimension 16's cell lacks. An unattributed report is not the Odyssey's epistemic condition; it is the failure of that condition.

And the second break, which is decisive. In Homer recognition arrives. Under C-31 four questions about the 2026 film are closed as permanently unresolved — no screening, no script, no library visit, ever. A mirror in which recognition can never occur is not a mirror of the Odyssey. It is a mirror of the suitors' Ithaca: a household reasoning confidently about a man it has decided will not return. The mirror reading is available, it is beautiful, and it licenses nothing — least of all a coordinate. Recognition-by-report is a condition to be shown, never a warrant to average.

Fault line: the hanged girls

The charge, and who is under it. Atwood put it in terms nobody at the table could soften: Book 22 "contains two slaughters, not one." The suitors are killed; twelve enslaved women are then made to clear the hall and are hanged for it, hanged specifically so they will not get a clean death. Her indictment of dimension 9 is procedural, not moral: if the concordance carries one row per episode, and the row at the position of the slaughter records the suitors and grades the book's answer as a schedule of remedies ranking hushmoney above assassination, then "the project has recorded half of a double occasion, and the half it dropped is the half done to women." That charge lands on dimension 9, and dimension 9 has no defender here. Ginzburg's knife: "Unless the coding rule for transacted was written down before the eighteen were graded, that nine is a number about the grader's degrees of freedom." This does not resolve, and the reason it does not resolve is Saramago's: the unit is unpublished. "If a letter counts, a photograph counts, an arm counts, and being the subject of men's talk counts, then mediation is generous by construction." Pamuk's perturbation test and Ginzburg's coding sheet are the same missing document, and until it exists nobody at this table can say whether the two axes were scored independently or whether one was scored off the other. Pamuk's robustness claim is therefore currently unsupported by anything but his confidence — a concession he half-makes himself when he says the Object Museum "publishes its catalogue and withholds its accession policy," which costs him the affect his own museum practice trades on.

Third exchange — three works, or two and a rumour? Galeano offers the comparison as safe: three works take three positions "on the same twelve lines, and only one of them looks," and because what is compared is the report rather than the film, marking it "costs nothing." Two objections break it.

First, factually. Homer looks. The poem narrates the hanging; it is the demotion the poem performs, not the aversion. Atwood's own framing is the correction — the twelve are counted and disposed of as housekeeping, which is worse than being unwatched. So Galeano's ordinal has the wrong shape at position one, and an ordinal with a wrong terminus is not a scale.

Second, evidentially. Saramago: "Refusing to update a number is a much smaller discipline than refusing to state one." Pamuk sharpens it to a structural fact about dimension 16 — the REPORTED Nolan column is the sole occupant of the ADD pole; remove it and the central contrast has one example on one side. He told the table the poem counts twelve of the household's fifty and names one, Melantho, then marked it himself: "I offer that as a reading, not a count." Correctly — this project has already struck two untraceable figures (12,110; "173 versus 18"). Every Homeric particular in this session is provisional. That concession costs Senn his own Book 24 orchard argument until someone prints the lines.

The straw Ginzburg. Atwood, Galeano and Saramago each broke Ginzburg on against-the-grain reading — a novel has no archival residue, Menocchio survives because a hostile institution wrote him down, this reader has no adversary. All three are right, and none of them was answering Ginzburg, who advanced no such transfer; he spent his opening demanding inclusion rules and declining Borges's compliment. The consequence matters more than the misfire: with recovery unclaimed by anyone, the register of the unnamed has no proposed epistemology. Nothing on this table can retrieve the servant girl at the pork butcher's counter, whom Atwood correctly identifies as Ulysses' own dmoe. Galeano states the terminal fact: against-the-grain reading of fiction "recovers Joyce's decision not to draw her, which is a different and lesser object."

FINDING. The comparison establishes one thing that survives, and it is not a comparison of the three works' merits. It is that at this single position four instruments fail in the same direction. Homer counts the twelve and does not name them. Wilson, whose stated policy is that softer words would imply freedom, writes "these girls" at the exact moment of the hanging — Pamuk's reading, and the finest instance of L-17 the project owns: a translator's own qualifier failing to reach her hardest scene, while her refusal holds: "I refuse to grant these girls a clean death." REPORTED and second-hand, permanently so under C-31, Nolan's film elides the hanging onto a single betrayer; no report is verified and none may be treated as established. And dimension 2 admits by name and therefore cannot reach the unnamed servant, which is L-38 with a class attached — Ginzburg: "the class it cannot reach is not random." The blind spot is at the same coordinate every time: the woman who is owned, counted, and not named. Senn supplies the long duration for it — Eurycleia, the slave who performs the first recognition and is throttled into silence by the man she recognises; Shneiderman supplies the mechanism — the weakest credential is read first by the person with least standing to act on it. The household's women are its evidence-bearers and its disposables, and that is one finding, not four.

What the comparison does not establish: anything about Ulysses' treatment of the hanging (unrun, and unprintable under A19); any ranking that includes the film; any strengthening of dimension 9.

The adversarial verifier

The verifier's report

2. Invented or garbled — one systemic error, repeated by seven voices.

The line "I refuse to grant these girls a clean death" is spoken in the poem by Telemachus. It is Wilson's rendering of a killer's stated cruelty, not a translator's statement about her own practice. The brief says only "Her line is…" — a rendering. The session escalates that into authorship, and does so most explicitly where it matters most:

  • Atwood: "What is verified is the refusal, in her own words: 'I refuse to grant these girls a clean death.'"
  • Pamuk: "a translator signing her arrangement in the first person, which is A10b performed by somebody else's book."
  • Borges: "a translator's act, not a lexical one."
  • Kenner commits it in the same paragraph in which he states the rule that forbids it — "a note is a voice, a passage is a voice, and they are not the same voice" — then: "What Book 22 does support is the other half — 'I refuse to grant these girls a clean death'."

This is the identical failure the project already caught once (policy lifted from the Note and dropped on the scene) performed in the opposite direction, and no voice catches it. A10b is void here; the "I" is a character's. Verify against Wilson 22 before anything ships.

Second: Shneiderman invents a project asset. "Ithaca is a query interface, and the project's own count — 303 question marks there, none at all in Penelope — is evidence it has not yet spent."
